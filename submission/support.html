Permissions

HealthKit, Motion & Fitness, and notifications are optional. The timer remains available if you decline these permissions, and you can enable them later in iOS Settings.

Widgets or Live Activity not updating

Open the app once after changing permissions or widget settings. Make sure Background App Refresh and Live Activities are enabled for Niko Neko Run.

Export or delete data

Open Settings → Data & Sync. Use Export CSV to share a copy, or Clear All Data to remove the local run log. HealthKit records are managed in Apple’s Health app.
