### Description

Niko Neko Run is a quiet, focused timer for slow jogging.

Open the app, choose a duration, and tap start. The screen keeps only what matters: time, beat, and the metrics you choose to see. No social feed, leaderboard, or crowded dashboard. Just enough information to keep your attention on your feet.

**A comfortable pace**

- Countdown or stopwatch mode, with plain minutes or HH:MM display
- 140–220 BPM metronome with multiple sounds and volume control
- Heart rate, distance, calories, and steps while you run (when permitted)
- Hold to stop, so an accidental tap does not erase a session

**A visible habit**

- Day, week, month, and year reports
- Duration, distance, calories, steps, heart rate, and cadence
- A calm completion summary and day-streak view
- CSV export so your log stays yours

**Close at hand**

- Widgets for stats, heatmaps, bar charts, calendars, and all metrics
- Dynamic Island and Lock Screen Live Activity
- 14 themes, Traditional Chinese, and English
- Selectable running characters for a little company on every run

Apple Health and Motion permissions are optional. The timer still works when permissions are declined; only the related metrics and Health write-back are disabled. Data is kept on your device by default. No account, ads, or subscription required.

Go slower. Keep going. Show up today.
