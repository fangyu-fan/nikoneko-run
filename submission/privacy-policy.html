Niko Neko Run Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-16 · Contact: [replace with your support email]

Niko Neko Run does not require an account and does not operate an advertising, analytics, or tracking service. Run records and preferences are stored locally on your device by default. We do not sell personal information.

Information the App can access

Only after you grant permission, the App can access HealthKit heart rate, body mass, height, and workout-related data; Motion & Fitness steps and distance; and the reminder permission/time needed for an optional local notification. These data are used to show your metrics, save your local record, create widgets and Live Activities, and write the workout data you explicitly enabled.

Storage and sharing

The local run log and settings are stored on the device using Apple system storage. CSV export is initiated by you and passed to the iOS share sheet; you choose the destination. The App has no required server account or social sharing service.

Your choices

You can revoke permissions in iOS Settings. Delete local records from Settings → Data & Sync → Clear All Data. HealthKit records written by the App are managed in Apple’s Health app.
