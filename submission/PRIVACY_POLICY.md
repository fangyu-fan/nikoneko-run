# Niko Neko Run Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-08-16  
**Contact:** `[replace with your support email]`

This policy describes how Niko Neko Run (“the App”) handles information when you use the iOS app.

## Summary

Niko Neko Run does not require an account and does not operate an advertising, analytics, or tracking service. Run records and preferences are stored locally on your device by default. We do not sell personal information.

## Information the App can access

Only when you grant the relevant iOS permission, the App can access:

- **HealthKit:** heart rate, body mass, height, and data needed to write a completed running workout, active energy, and walking/running distance.
- **Motion & Fitness:** step count and distance during an active run. Calories and cadence are calculated locally from the available measurements and your profile settings.
- **Notifications:** the reminder time and permission state needed to schedule an optional local reminder.

The App uses these data only to show your run metrics, save your local run record, create widgets/Live Activities, and write the workout data you explicitly enabled. Health and fitness data is not used for advertising, sold, or shared with data brokers.

## Storage and sharing

The local run log and settings are stored on the device using Apple system storage. CSV export is initiated by you and is passed to the iOS share sheet; the destination is chosen by you. The App has no required server account or social sharing service.

## Your choices

You can deny or later revoke HealthKit, Motion, and Notification permissions in iOS Settings. You can delete local run records from **Settings → Data & Sync → Clear All Data**. Deleting the App removes its local data according to iOS behavior. HealthKit data written by the App is managed in the Health app and can be deleted there.

## Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the App’s data practices change. The effective date above will be updated with each revision. Questions can be sent to the support contact listed at the top of this policy.
